Privacy Policy: Christmas Toy Factory Idle

Effective date: 2026-11-15
Developer: Northbeam ("we", "us")
Contact: northbeamgames@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game Christmas Toy Factory Idle (the "Game", package com.northbeam.christmastoyfactory) handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version distributed on Google Play.

1. The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We don't run game servers and we don't receive your save file.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • The only purchase is an optional "Remove Ads" item, processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Gems (the in-game premium currency) are earned by playing and by watching optional rewarded ads. They are never sold for real money.

2. Information stored on your device only

The Game stores your progress locally, in the app's private storage on your phone: coins, gems, levels, globes, helpers, decorations, settings and the time you last played (used to calculate offline earnings). Your Remove Ads status is also stored locally. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app's data. We have no copy and can't restore it.

The Game uses your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to detect when you shake the device. Motion readings are processed instantly on the device to start the in-game snowstorm. They are not stored or transmitted. The accelerometer needs no permission.

3. Advertising: Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform

We use Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and UK) to show:

  • rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus (a 4-hour 2× Blizzard boost, doubled offline earnings, a ×3 golden snowflake, or a few gems up to a small daily limit), and
  • occasional interstitial (full-screen) ads at natural breaks in play, unless you've bought Remove Ads.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the Game automatically collects and shares the following with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google's AdMob data disclosure:

  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID (where available and permitted), the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views (including video views).
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the Game and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google's use of it is governed by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regulated regions). When you first open the Game in a region where consent is required, Google's User Messaging Platform shows a consent message before any ad is requested. You can accept or decline personalized ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the Game (this row appears wherever a consent choice applies). Where US state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, that option is offered in the same place.

Your advertising ID controls. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

We don't sell your personal information for money. We don't receive or store the identifiers AdMob collects. We see only aggregated reports, such as ad impressions and earnings, in our AdMob account.

4. In-app purchase: Remove Ads

You can optionally buy a one-time "Remove Ads" item. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and billing information under the Google Payments privacy notice. The Game receives only a confirmation from Google Play that the item is owned, and uses it on your device to turn ads off and to restore the purchase if you reinstall. We don't run a purchase server and we don't receive your name, email or payment details.

Gems and coins are never sold for real money. Gems are earned by playing (challenges, daily goals, achievements and other in-game rewards) and by watching optional rewarded ads. Watching an ad for gems is always your choice and is limited to a few times per day.

5. Analytics

The current version of the Game doesn't use a separate analytics service. Game events are counted only on your device and are not transmitted. The Google Mobile Ads SDK's own diagnostics (section 3) are the only usage data that leaves your device. If we add an analytics provider in a future update, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that update is released.

8. Links that open your browser

Tapping an ad or the Privacy policy row in Settings opens your web browser (or a browser tab inside the Game). What happens on the website you visit is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

9. Children

The Game is designed for and directed to a general audience aged 13 and older. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the Game in a way that resulted in data being collected by our advertising partner, contact us at northbeamgames@gmail.com and we'll help you reset the advertising ID and stop ad data collection on that device. Because we hold no account or save data on our side, there is nothing stored by us to delete.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress: stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → Christmas Toy Factory Idle → Storage → Clear data).
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its own policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time. To ask Google about data it holds, see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play under its own terms. You can see your order history in your Google account.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because we don't store personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through the in-game Privacy options, your device's ad settings, or Google directly. You can always contact us at northbeamgames@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Legal bases (EEA and UK): consent, for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for ads where required; our legitimate interests, for non-personalized ad delivery, fraud prevention and keeping the Game working; and performance of a contract, for delivering the Remove Ads purchase.

12. International transfers

Google may process advertising and purchase data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's policies.

13. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing is encrypted in transit. Your local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read on a standard Android device.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and post the new version at this address. Significant changes, such as adding a new data-collecting service or permission, will also be noted in the Game's release notes.
